# Participant Quick Guide

## What you will do

1. Join one shared room.
2. Answer a live first-search poll.
3. Learn how people reduce uncertainty through search.
4. Learn the five phases: Spark, Search, Compare, Prove, Act & Echo.
5. Match search moments to the right surfaces.
6. Take one turn on the participant wheel.
7. Spend 100 visibility credits for Breezy or K&P.
8. Complete one client micro-mission.
9. Build one human → machine → business evidence chain.
10. Add one roadmap move and vote on priorities.
11. Explain Search Everywhere in client-ready language.

## Workshop rules

- Short answers are expected.
- You are not conducting a full audit.
- One useful sentence is enough.
- Recommend a platform only when it fits the audience moment.
- Label hypotheses as hypotheses.
- Name the likely execution owner for offsite actions.
- Do not enter confidential client or customer information.

## Five-phase journey

- **Spark:** A problem, desire, recommendation, or deadline appears.
- **Search:** The person frames the need and finds starting options.
- **Compare:** The person evaluates meaningful differences.
- **Prove:** The person tests trust and looks for independent evidence.
- **Act & Echo:** The person acts, returns, reviews, or creates the next search.
